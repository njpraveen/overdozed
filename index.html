हमारी कथाThe Story

Ancient wisdom meets modern streetwear.

Overdozed began in a Jaipur block-printing workshop, sketching the carvings of the Meenakshi and Brihadeeswarar gopurams into notebooks. We wanted the gods we grew up with on clothes we would actually wear out.

Each design starts as traditional line art reviewed with temple artisans, then gets a neon treatment. The deity is always shown with respect: no jokes, no distortion, nothing printed below the waist.
